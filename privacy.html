This policy describes Along’s running app for iPhone and Apple Watch and its support website. Some features depend on your installed beta version.

01

Running without an account

You can record solo workouts without an Along account. Along uses permissions you grant through Apple’s system controls to record workouts, access relevant Apple Health information, and record outdoor routes. Workout records, precise routes, exact heart rate, calories and private heart-rate zone settings are processed on your devices and, where authorized, in Apple Health. Along does not send precise routes, exact heart rate or calories to its social backend.

Apple controls any synchronization or backup performed by its services under your device settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

02

Optional accounts and running with someone

If you choose social features, Along uses Sign in with Apple and Supabase to authenticate your account. This can include your Apple account identifier and information Apple supplies, such as an email address or private relay address. Along stores your chosen display name and optional profile thumbnail, your connections and blocks, availability, invitations, and shared-run coordination and result records.

Your profile is available to you and authorized connections. A person reviewing your valid private connection invitation can see the information needed to decide whether to connect. Along does not provide public profile discovery.

During a shared run, Along transmits distance, pace and run state to the authorized participant. Heart-rate zone sharing is optional for each connection. Exact heart rate, calories and route coordinates are not included. Shared summaries let participants review their run. Blocking or removing a connection restricts future access according to the app’s connection rules; it cannot erase information another person has already seen or copied.

03

Notifications and invitation links

If you enable invitation alerts, Along registers an Apple push token with its backend and uses Apple Push Notification service to send generic invitation prompts. The backend stores device registration and delivery-attempt information. Turning off alerts does not disable invitations inside the app.

Private connection codes and links are invitation capabilities. Share them only with the intended recipient. The app can retain a pending invitation securely on the device until use, dismissal or expiry. Opening a link does not automatically accept a connection or start a workout.

04

Diagnostics and support

Along keeps limited diagnostic events locally, including build and system versions, timing, device state and categorized outcomes. The local log is limited to seven days and 1 MiB. It excludes names, invitation codes, authentication tokens, route coordinates and measured health values. You choose whether to export or share it; Along does not automatically upload this local diagnostic log.

If you email support or share a report, I receive the information you send and use it to respond and investigate the issue. Apple may separately provide beta feedback or crash information through its developer services according to your Apple settings and participation in TestFlight.

05

Service providers and website visits

Along uses Apple for sign-in, Health integration, distribution and notifications, and Supabase for account and social services. These services process information needed to provide their functions, including technical connection and operational information.

The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical information when you visit it under the GitHub privacy statement. The current website template also loads a page-navigation script from cdnjs, so your browser contacts that provider. Along does not add advertising or behavioral tracking to the app.

06

Retention and deletion

Account, profile and social records support your account, connections and shared history. Expired availability or an unusable invitation does not necessarily mean its underlying record has already been erased.

Account deletion removes your account and associated profile data, including the stored profile photo and push registrations. A partner’s own workout record can remain with your identity removed. Deletion does not remove local workout history or Apple Health records, nor copies of information others already hold. Manage Apple Health data separately in Apple’s apps and settings.

Local pending invitations expire, and restored notification intents are used only for short-lived review. Push delivery records older than seven days are eligible for cleanup during later delivery activity; this is not a guaranteed deletion exactly seven days after creation. Provider logs and backups have separate retention arrangements.

07

Your choices and requests

You can use solo running without a social account, change your profile, manage connections and zone sharing, disable notifications, and change Health or location permissions in Apple’s settings. Revoking permission can limit the features that depend on it.

Use Along’s account controls to export your account data or request account deletion. If a beta feature is unavailable or a request fails, email me for help. You can also contact me to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, including support correspondence. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights to portability, restriction or objection and to complain to a data-protection authority.
